# EU AI Act: when the obligations apply

## Answer

The Article 5 prohibitions, as originally adopted, have applied since 2 February 2025 [1][9]. Obligations for general-purpose AI (GPAI) models have applied since 2 August 2025, and the Commission's enforcement powers over them, including fines, start on 2 August 2026 [4][2]. Whether those fines can reach conduct before that date is disputed (see "General-purpose AI models") [12][13]. The Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, entered into force on 27 July 2026 and delayed high-risk obligations [5][3]: Annex III high-risk systems now apply from 2 December 2027 (originally 2 August 2026), and Annex I product-embedded high-risk systems from 2 August 2028 (originally 2 August 2027) [1][3][7].

The new prohibitions on non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material apply from 2 December 2026 [1][2].

## Dates by category

- Prohibited practices (Art. 5), as originally adopted: 2 February 2025 [1][9].
- Amended Article 5(1) points (ba) and (bb), and Article 5(1a) and (1b): 2 December 2026 [1].
- New prohibitions (non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM generators): 2 December 2026 [1][2][9].
- GPAI model obligations: 2 August 2025 [4].
- Commission enforcement powers over GPAI models, including fines: 2 August 2026 [4][2].
- Penalty provisions (Chapter XII, titled "Penalties", including Article 99): 2 August 2025, with Article 101 excepted [1][15]. Fines for GPAI providers are therefore enforced from 2 August 2026 [4][2]. The scope of the penalties article is not stated here, because I did not read its text.
- The Act otherwise applies from 2 August 2026, the general date in Article 113 [1].
- Article 50 transparency: 2 August 2026 [2].
- High-risk systems, Annex III: 2 December 2027 (originally 2 August 2026) [1][3][7]. Future; delayed [1][3][7].
- High-risk systems, Annex I (embedded in regulated products): 2 August 2028 (originally 2 August 2027) [1][3][7]. Future; delayed [1][3][7].

## Prohibited practices

Article 113 sets the application dates. Chapters I and II apply from 2 February 2025, except the new Article 5(1) points (ba) and (bb) and Article 5(1a) and (1b), which apply from 2 December 2026 [1]. The Commission lists nine prohibited practices, says the first eight became effective in February 2025, and says the ninth comes into effect in December 2026 [9]. The ninth covers AI systems that generate non-consensual sexually explicit and intimate content or child sexual abuse material [3][9].

## General-purpose AI models

The obligations for GPAI providers entered into application on 2 August 2025 [4]. Under Article 113(b), Chapter V, Chapter VII, Chapter XII and Article 78 apply from that date, with the exception of Article 101 [1][15]. The Commission says that from 2 August 2026 its enforcement powers enter into application, and that it "will enforce compliance with the obligations for providers of GPAI models, including with fines" [4]. Article 101 allows fines "not exceeding 3 % of their annual total worldwide turnover in the preceding financial year or EUR 15 000 000, whichever is higher" [11]. Providers of GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must comply by 2 August 2027 [4].

Whether the fine power reaches earlier conduct is not settled by the text. Praesidia says "The text does not say whether Article 101 reaches an infringement that ended before that date" [13]. Bratby Law quotes the Commission's guidelines of 19 November 2025: "In the first year from 2 August 2025 onwards, the Commission cannot take any enforcement actions because its enforcement powers only enter into application on 2 August 2026" [12]. Bratby Law reads this as meaning suppliers must be compliant on 2 August 2026 [12]. This report dates the start of fines to 2 August 2026 [4]. The Commission GPAI page, as read, does not address retroactivity [4].

## High-risk AI systems

Under amended Article 113(c), Chapter III, Sections 1, 2 and 3 (with the exception of Article 6(5)) apply to Annex III systems from 2 December 2027 and to Annex I systems from 2 August 2028 [1]. The Commission's entry-into-force page gives the same dates: "Rules apply starting 2 December 2027" for Annex III and "Rules apply starting 2 August 2028" for Annex I [3]. Before amendment, the dates were 2 August 2026 for Annex III and 2 August 2027 for Annex I [7].

The agreed text replaced the Commission's proposed conditional delay mechanism with fixed dates [7].

## Other dates in the amended Act

- Articles 102 to 110 apply from 27 July 2026 [1].
- Certain providers of AI systems, including GPAI systems, that generate synthetic content and were already placed on the market before 2 August 2026 have a transitional deadline of 2 December 2026 to comply with Article 50(2) [2].
- Member States should have at least one national AI regulatory sandbox operational by 2 August 2027 [2].

## Amendment history

- **Proposal:** The Commission proposed the Digital Omnibus on AI on 19 November 2025 [3].
- **Provisional agreement:** The Council and Parliament announced a provisional agreement on 7 May 2026 [8]. Another account gives 6 May, confirmed by Council representatives on 13 May [7].
- **Parliament:** On 16 June 2026, Parliament gave final approval [6][10]. Liberties reports 423 votes in favour [10]. Sofia Globe reports 57 against and 174 abstentions [6]. Only Sofia Globe gives those counts [6]. I found no second source for them.
- **Council:** A Council press release dated 29 June 2026 is cited by Law & Technology [5]. I did not read the press release itself.
- **Publication and entry into force:** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, dated 8 July 2026, was published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026 [5][3].
- **Corrigendum:** An EUR-Lex record dated 29 September 2026 is a corrigendum that corrects Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 [14]. The record shows it is in German. I did not read its text, so I cannot say what it changes; I found no evidence that it changes any date in this report.

The amendment also extends some SME simplifications to small mid-caps, expands regulatory sandboxes, simplifies the AI literacy duty, and adds the ban on nudification apps [3].

## Where sources disagree

- **Article 101 start date.** The artificialintelligenceact.eu page for Article 101 says it "Comes into force 2 August 2025, according to Article 113(b)" [11]. Article 113(b) excludes Article 101 from that date [1], and the Commission ties fines to enforcement from 2 August 2026 [4][2]. On that basis, this report uses 2 August 2026 for fines [4][2]. The 2 August 2026 date is the one given by the Commission [4].
- **Watermarking.** Sofia Globe says the law "delays the application of watermarking obligations on AI-generated content until December 2 2026" [6]. The Commission timeline is narrower: Article 50 applies from 2 August 2026, and only providers of systems already on the market before that date have until 2 December 2026 for Article 50(2) [2]. This report follows the Commission.
- **Date of the provisional agreement.** One account gives 6 May [7]. Another gives 7 May [8].

## Limits

- The amended Article 113 is not checked against the Official Journal text. The only EUR-Lex/Cellar page I read is a metadata record for the corrigendum [14], not the regulation text, and I did not read the regulation's body on EUR-Lex. The statutory wording comes from artificialintelligenceact.eu, and the dates are cross-checked against the Commission and the AI Act Service Desk [1][2][3].
- I did not read the Council or Parliament press releases.
- I did not review the Commission's guidance on high-risk classification, or the detailed Annex I changes.
- I found no later amendment that moves these dates, but I did not search EUR-Lex for later changes or corrigenda. Check EUR-Lex before any compliance decision.
- The data-protection Digital Omnibus is a separate proposal, still under negotiation in the Council, and is distinct from the AI Omnibus [5].
