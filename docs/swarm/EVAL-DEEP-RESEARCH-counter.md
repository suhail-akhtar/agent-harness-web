## Art. 5 prohibitions apply since 2 February 2025 (as originally adopted)
- Verdict: holds
- Evidence against (or "none found"): none found. Supporting primary text: "Prohibitions 1-8 became effective in February 2025." - https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai (European Commission, DG CNECT, undated page). Also "These prohibitions have been in place since 2 February 2025." - https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/EN/Areas/Digitalisation/AI/08_ProhibitedPractices/start.html (Bundesnetzagentur, undated).
- Minor counting dispute (does not change any date): the Commission page lists "nine prohibited practices", while the aiactguide guide says Art. 5(1) "holds ten lettered points" once (ba) and (bb) are counted, and notes the Commission counts nine - https://aiactguide.net/digital-omnibus/ (AIActGuide.net, updated 25 October 2026).

## Commission enforcement powers over GPAI, including fines, start 2 August 2026
- Verdict: holds (with one contested reading on the start of penalties, see evidence)
- Evidence against (or "none found"):
  - Contested reading, Art. 99 penalty rules: "The penalty provisions, including the Article 99(3) fine ceiling, since 2 August 2025 (Article 113(a) and (b))." - https://praesidia.ai/blog/eu-ai-act-gpai-enforcement-live-august-2026 (Praesidia, updated 2 October 2026). This concerns national penalty rules for prohibited practices, not GPAI fines under Art. 101, so it does not contradict the report's 2 August 2026 date for Art. 101 fines. The aiactguide guide also lists "Chapters ... XII and Article 78 apply since 2 August 2025" (point (b)) - https://aiactguide.net/digital-omnibus/. The report does not state a penalties date, so this is a gap rather than a conflict.
  - Contested reading, retroactivity: a commentary says "fines reach back to August 2025", which Bratby Law calls going "further than the published material supports" - https://bratby.law/gpai-enforcement-ai-office-fines/ (Bratby Law, c. August 2026). Bratby quotes the Commission's guidelines of 19 Nov 2025: "In the first year from 2 August 2025 onwards, the Commission cannot take any enforcement actions because its enforcement powers only enter into application on 2 August 2026." Praesidia also says "Whether that power reaches conduct before that date is not settled by the text" - https://praesidia.ai/blog/eu-ai-act-gpai-enforcement-live-august-2026. The report's reading is supported by the Commission's own guidance; the retroactivity question is open in commentary, not in the Commission's text.
  - Article 101 start-date conflict already noted in the report: artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/101 says "Comes into force 2 August 2025" (cited in report). Not re-fetched here.
- Primary support: "From 2 August 2026, the Commission's enforcement powers enter into application. The Commission will enforce compliance with the obligations for providers of GPAI models, including with fines." - https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-gpai-providers (European Commission). The Commission press release of 31 July 2026 is cited by Bratby (ip_26_1714); not fetched.
- Searched: "general-purpose AI code of practice enforcement August 2026 AI Office fines begin"; "AI Office fines general-purpose models cannot apply retroactively conduct before 2 August 2026 non-retroactivity". Fetched: Commission GPAI page, Bratby Law, Praesidia.

## Digital Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) in force 27 July 2026; Annex III high-risk moved to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028
- Verdict: holds
- Evidence against (or "none found"):
  - No source found disputing the dates. Critics object to the delay itself, not the date: "The adopted text will weaken AI Act protections, delay the enforcement of key provisions, and empower industry actors." - https://www.liberties.eu/en/stories/ai-omnibus-ep-vote/45715 (Liberties, 17 June 2026). This is a policy objection; it does not dispute the dates.
  - Support, Commission: "AI Omnibus enters into force... High-risk AI systems in Annex III: Rules apply starting 2 December 2027. High-risk AI embedded in physical products... in Annex I: Rules apply starting 2 August 2028." - https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/ai-omnibus-enters-force (European Commission, last update 31 July 2026). "On 27 July 2026, the AI Omnibus enters into force across the EU."
  - Support, OJ-based secondary guide: "Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 ... published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026... (Article 4)" and "Before 27 July 2026, the same sections were to apply from 2 August 2026 and 2 August 2027." - https://aiactguide.net/digital-omnibus/ (AIActGuide.net, 25 October 2026).
  - Corrigendum: "The single corrigendum of 29 September 2026 corrects only the German wording of Article 6(1b)... and changes no date." - https://aiactguide.net/digital-omnibus/. The report does not mention this corrigendum; it does not affect the dates.
  - Risk to the date (not a contradiction): the aiactguide says "EN 18286:2026... is the only harmonised standard cited under the AI Act as of 24 October 2026" and the Art. 6(5) guidelines exist only as a draft of 19 May 2026. These are reasons the Annex III date could be pressed again, but no source reports a further delay.
  - Parliament vote 423 in favour: confirmed by Liberties (16 June 2026). The report's Council and provisional-agreement dates (6/7 May) were not checked here.
- Searched: "AI Act high-risk obligations postponed December 2027 digital omnibus criticism"; "Digital Omnibus AI Act amendment Official Journal 2026 corrigendum"; "civil society criticises EU AI Act delay high-risk rules omnibus weakens protections"; "AI Act further delay proposal November 2026 Parliament Council new amendment" (results were mostly older items on the first deal; no new delay found); "Regulation 2026/1744 Article 113 high-risk '2 December 2027' text Official Journal L 2026/1744".
- Not checked: Article 113 text read directly from EUR-Lex (blocked, HTTP 202); Council press release of 29 June 2026; the separate data-protection Digital Omnibus COM(2025) 837 (aiactguide says it is pending and does not amend the AI Act).
